# Day 1 - Salesforce CRM Basics

## Topics Covered
- What is Salesforce CRM
- Customer 360 Overview
- Salesforce Platform Basics
- Data Model Basics
- Leads, Contacts, Accounts & Opportunities

## Key Points
- Salesforce helps businesses manage customer relationships efficiently.
- Customer 360 connects sales, marketing, and support teams on one platform.
- Leads represent unqualified opportunities.
- Leads can be converted into:
  - Accounts
  - Contacts
  - Opportunities
- Salesforce uses objects, fields, and records to organize data.
- Automation and AI improve productivity and workflow management.

## What I Learned

- Understanding of CRM workflow
- Importance of real-time customer data
- Salesforce object relationships and lifecycle management

## Conclusion
Today’s learning provided a beginner-friendly introduction to Salesforce CRM, Customer 360, and Salesforce platform fundamentals.
